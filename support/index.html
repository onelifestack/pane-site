A widget stopped updating

Some phones pause apps in the background, which freezes widgets. Open Pane, go to Settings and follow the short guide for your brand (Xiaomi, Samsung, Vivo, OnePlus, Realme or Pixel). It is one setting: allow Pane to run without battery restrictions.

I cannot find Pane in the widget list

Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, then scroll to Pane. Some launchers list widgets alphabetically by app name, and some have a search box. The "Add" button inside the app can only ask your launcher to add a widget; some launchers accept the request without placing one, so use the widget list.

Change or remove a widget

Open Pane, tap On home, and tap Edit to change the look. To remove a widget, press and hold it on your home screen and choose Remove.

Delete my data

Pane keeps your widget choices only on your phone. To delete them, clear Pane's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. We hold no copy.
