Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Pane is a set of home-screen widgets for Android. In short: Pane collects nothing, sends nothing and asks for no permissions.

What Pane does not do

  • It has no account, sign-in or profile.
  • It does not ask for any Android permission. It has no internet access, so it cannot send anything to anyone.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • We, OneLifeStack, receive no information about you or your phone from the app.

What stays on your phone

Pane saves your choices (for example the glass, text colour, strength and corners of each widget, and whether you have finished the welcome screens) in its own private storage on your phone. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes them.

The widgets show your phone's own time, date and battery level. Pane reads the battery level from Android, which does not need a permission, and never stores it.

Wallpapers

The four wallpapers are drawn on your phone. When you tap "Set as wallpaper", Pane saves the picture in its private cache and hands it to Android's own "set wallpaper" screen. It does not read your photos or your current wallpaper.

Google Play

If you install Pane from Google Play, Google may collect its own information about the install, such as install counts and crash reports that you have chosen to share with Google. That is Google's, under Google's privacy policy; Pane does not see it.

Children

Pane collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds an optional feature that needs a permission, we will update this page and the Play listing before that version ships. Questions: hello@onelifestack.com.
